Welcome to Catpedia

From ancient origins to modern-day companions, cats have shaped human culture for centuries. This guide explores their history, personality, and the breeds that continue to capture our hearts.

Whenever I spend time with my cat, Bean, or watch a particularly dramatic feline video, I start asking bigger questions: where did cats originate, how did they become domesticated, and why do they have such a powerful place in our homes and culture?

This website covers the history of cats and highlights several popular breeds, each with its own distinct personality and charm.
