Maine Coon

Personality

Known as “gentle giants,” they are affectionate, intelligent, and playful, often enjoying water and learning tricks.

Black Cat

Personality

Affectionate, social, and deeply loyal companions with a curious streak and even a chatty side.

Tabby Cat

Personality

Friendly, adaptable, and often bold, tabbies tend to be active, affectionate, and endlessly curious.

British Shorthair

Personality

Calm, easygoing, and low-maintenance, they are affectionate without being overly demanding.

Siamese Cat

Personality

Highly social, vocal, and deeply bonded to their people, Siamese cats thrive on interaction and attention.

Tuxedo Cat

Personality

Intelligent, charismatic, and full of personality, tuxedo cats are often affectionate and delightfully expressive.

Orange Cat

Personality

Known for their energetic, playful nature, orange cats are often affectionate, social, and a little chaotic.

Ragdoll Cat

Personality

Gentle, laid-back, and people-focused, Ragdolls are famous for their relaxed temperament and puppy-like loyalty.

Munchkin Cat

Personality

Outgoing, affectionate, and playful, Munchkins are curious little explorers with a surprisingly big personality.
