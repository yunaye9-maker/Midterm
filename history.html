History of Cats

This image shows the African wildcat, Felis silvestris lybica, which evolved into the cats we know today, Felis catus. Cats are among the most popular pets on Earth, kept on every continent except Antarctica. While some may debate the exact count, these adaptable animals have lived alongside people for thousands of years.

Domestic cats evolved from African wildcats, a relationship that began in ancient Mesopotamia, where they helped protect grain stores from rodents. Over time, the bond between people and cats strengthened, leading to their place in homes, mythology, and culture across the world.

Throughout history, cats have been revered, feared, and admired. Their quiet independence, sharp instincts, and affectionate nature helped secure their status as both working animals and beloved companions.
